# Out-of-Home (OOH) Advertising Domain Intelligence

This skill equips the agent with industry-standard rules, financial math, and operational practices for outdoor media advertising (Heera Advertising / AdHoardings).

---

## 1. Industry Terminology & Standards

- **Hoarding / Billboard**: Large outdoor advertising structure typically placed in high-traffic commercial arteries and highway junctions.
- **Illumination Types**:
  - **Front Lit**: External spotlights shining onto the flex surface. Most popular and cost-effective.
  - **Back Lit**: Light boxes with lights inside shining through translucent vinyl. Premium night visibility.
  - **Non Lit**: Daylight-only visibility. Budget tier.
- **OOH Metrics**:
  - **DEC (Daily Effective Circulation)**: Estimated vehicle + pedestrian count passing the site within a 12-hour or 18-hour window.
  - **OTS (Opportunity to See)**: Probability of the target audience having an unobstructed line of sight.
  - **Visibility Angle**: Facing (Toward City Center, Toward Highway, Left/Right Roadside).

---

## 2. Commercial Math & Taxation

- **GST Rate**: Outdoor Advertising is taxed at **18% GST** under **SAC Code 998361** (Advertising agency services / sale of advertising space).
- **Pro-Rata Rental Formula**:
  \text{Rental} = \left(\frac{\text{Monthly Rate}}{30}\right) \times \text{Days Booked}
- **Printing & Mounting Charges (Pass-through)**:
  - Flex Printing: ₹8 to ₹14 per sq. ft.
  - Star Flex / Vinyl: ₹15 to ₹25 per sq. ft.
  - Mounting & Labor: ₹2 to ₹5 per sq. ft.
- **Occupancy Rate**:
  \text{Occupancy} = \left(\frac{\text{Occupied Sites}}{\text{Total Active Sites}}\right) \times 100

---

## 3. Operations & Proof of Execution (POE)

- **Daily / Periodic Audits**: Geo-tagged photos proving the client banner is mounted without tears, graffiti, or light failure.
- **Renewal Cycle**: Alert triggers **15 days** before slot release date to initiate retention pitches or remarket the site.
